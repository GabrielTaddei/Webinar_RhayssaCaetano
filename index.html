Aluminum chloride acts only at the tissue surface, unlike vasoconstrictors such as adrenaline. Per Kulzer, it has the thinnest cannula and highest viscosity among the syringe pastes they benchmarked.

Why we went cordless

In both cases we used no retraction cord

With a retraction cord

SizeCutSoakPlace

With RetraXil blue

Apply2 minRinse
  • Excellent hemostasis and tissue displacement before the impression
  • Fewer steps: no cord sizing, cutting or soaking
  • Less trauma and pressure on the tissue, more comfortable for the patient
  • Compatible with Flexitime: no setting inhibition (Kulzer test)
Key tip: rinse thoroughly with air-water spray and suction. Leftover astringent can affect the impression set. Do not exceed 2 minutes in the sulcus.
